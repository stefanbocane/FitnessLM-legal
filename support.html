Before you write

  • Data not syncing between devices? Open Settings → Storage & Sync in the app. Both devices must be signed into the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive on.
  • On-device AI won't load? Open AI Provider Settings to check this iPhone’s eligibility and model-download requirements. Some devices require an explicit local-AI beta opt-in. Keep the app open while local Coach work runs. Coach supports text; photo analysis is not available in this version. You can alternatively configure an external AI provider; provider access and an API key may be required.
  • Social account on hold? Email us with the subject "Social appeal" and the username involved.
  • Delete your account? Use Settings → Account → Delete Account. The app deletes the server account first, then resets local app data, credentials and preferences. If server deletion fails, your local data is preserved. If you see “Local Wipe Incomplete,” use Settings → Export Data → Reset All Data to finish. Email support if an error persists or shared content remains.
  • Scrolling feels choppy? Tell us which screen, whether Coach was processing, and whether the phone felt hot. A short recording can help; hide private information first.
  • Which profile fields are optional? In build 139, nickname, height, weight and exercise restrictions are optional. Age is required. Body-fat entry has been removed.
  • Control workout sharing? Open Social settings to review Auto-Share destinations and the separate Sync top lifts control.
  • Report content or a user? Use the in-app report or block controls, or email support with the subject “Safety report” and the relevant username or content details.

If data appears missing, contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app. Do not reset your only copy of workout history to troubleshoot.

Include in your message

  • iPhone model and iOS version
  • App version (Settings → bottom of the screen)
  • What you expected and what happened
  • For AI problems: local or external provider, and the error shown

Do not email API keys, passwords or a full data export. Share only the details needed to describe the problem.
